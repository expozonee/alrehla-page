Alrehla

An app for browsing scheduled bus trips and booking seats on them.

What you can do in the app

  • Browse available trips by city and starting point, without signing in.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google when you want to book.
  • Book a seat for yourself and add companions, based on available capacity.
  • Join the waitlist when a bus is full; your booking is confirmed automatically when a seat opens up.
  • Follow your upcoming bookings, change the number of companions, or cancel a booking.
  • Get notifications when a booking is confirmed, changed, or cancelled.

The app has no payments or in-app purchases.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can browse trips without signing in. You only need to sign in with Apple or Google to create a booking or manage your bookings.

How do I book a seat?

Choose your city and starting point, pick a trip, tap the booking button, and set the number of companions if any.

What is the waitlist?

If a trip doesn't have enough seats, you can join its waitlist. When a suitable seat becomes available, your booking is confirmed automatically and you get a notification.

How do I change companions or cancel a booking?

Open the "My Bookings" (حجوزاتي) page, select the booking, then change the number of companions or cancel it.

I'm not getting notifications. What should I do?

Make sure notifications for Alrehla are allowed in your device settings and that you are signed in to the app.

Are there payments in the app?

No. The app has no payments or in-app purchases.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time, or request deletion by email. Full details are on the Delete Account page.
